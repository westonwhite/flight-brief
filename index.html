Airspace & departure

• Tampa Class B starts at 3,000 ft over KCLW and 6,000 ft offshore by Egmont Key. Depart west to the beach and fly south along the shore at or below 2,500. Past Egmont, climb to 5,500 (under the 6,000 floor). Once south of about Anna Maria you're clear of the Class B and can climb to 9,500. You could also call Tampa Approach on 125.3 right after takeoff for flight following and a Class B climb.

• KPIE Class C is 6 nm southeast and MacDill (KMCF) Class D is 15 nm southeast. The westbound departure keeps you clear of both.

• Sarasota Class C runs from 1,200 to 4,000 ft. At 5,500 or higher you're above it, or you can talk to Tampa Approach on 119.65.

• Fort Myers Class C is well inland of the Captiva shoreline. Fort Myers Approach is 119.75 (west); Naples Tower is 128.5 and its Class D tops out at 2,500.

• South of Naples, use Miami Center for flight following (133.5 is the Key West RCAG near the Keys). KMTH is non-towered: CTAF 122.975, ASOS 135.525, left traffic, runway 7/25 is 5,008 × 100 ft.

• Special-use airspace: the W-174 warning areas are offshore, well west of the shoreline route. R-2916 (Cudjoe Key balloon, surface to 14,000) and the NAS Key West TFR (surface to 5,000) are 25+ nm west of Marathon. Avon Park R-2901 and the Lake Placid/Basinger MOAs are inland and not a factor.

• TFR: the Florida International Air Show at Punta Gorda (KPGD) has a TFR 5 nm around the airport, surface to 10,000 ft, Fri 11:00 AM–2:30 PM EST. The coastal route stays about 15 nm west of it, so don't cut inland. The Palm Beach/Homestead TFRs are off route.

• NOTAMs: the FAA NOTAM search blocked this server, so KCLW and KMTH NOTAMs were not checked. Check them in your official briefing.

• Over water: the shoreline route keeps you within gliding distance of land almost the whole way. The Everglades below the X01–Cape Sable leg is swamp with no good landing spots, and Florida Bay is about 24 nm of shallow water. Carry life vests and file a VFR flight plan.
